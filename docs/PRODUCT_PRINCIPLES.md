# Product principles

1. **Respect the subject.** Sacred material is presented with care, context, and attribution.
2. **Show the reasoning.** Explanations and Panchanga results expose relevant sources and assumptions.
3. **Keep traditions distinct.** Variations are represented instead of silently merged into one answer.
4. **Prefer clarity to spectacle.** The product should feel calm, legible, and useful.
5. **Design for inquiry.** Users can move from a concise answer to deeper sources and related concepts.
6. **Protect people.** Minimize collection of personal data and make sensitive operations explicit.
