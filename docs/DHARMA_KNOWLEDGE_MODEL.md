# Dharma knowledge model

The knowledge model must preserve both meaning and provenance.

## Core entities

- **Work:** a text, teaching, commentary, or oral tradition record.
- **Passage:** a bounded portion of a work with its original-language form and translations.
- **Concept:** a named idea, practice, person, place, or event.
- **Tradition:** the lineage, school, region, or interpretive context.
- **Interpretation:** an attributed explanation linked to one or more passages.
- **Source:** bibliographic or archival evidence and its edition details.

## Modeling rules

- Distinguish a quotation from an interpretation and from generated commentary.
- Preserve original language, transliteration, translation, and translator attribution separately.
- Represent disagreement and regional variation as first-class relationships.
- Never infer that absence from the model means nonexistence in the tradition.
- Every user-facing claim should be traceable to one or more sources or explicitly marked as generated.
