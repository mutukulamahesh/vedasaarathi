# Product vision

VedaSaarathi helps people engage with dharma knowledge and Panchanga information with clarity, context, and respect.

## Desired outcome

Users should be able to find dependable explanations, understand where a teaching comes from, and receive Panchanga results that make their location, timezone, and calculation assumptions clear.

## Product qualities

- Trustworthy rather than authoritative by assertion
- Contextual rather than flattened across traditions
- Useful for both discovery and deliberate study
- Accessible without stripping away nuance
- Transparent about sources, calculations, and uncertainty
